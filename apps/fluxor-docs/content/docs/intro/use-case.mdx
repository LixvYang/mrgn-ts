Fluxor Mixin Bot ID: 7000105323, also you can search by keywords **"借"** **"flux"** **"fluxor"**

You need to complete the Computer registration before using Fluxor

> You can send message to the bot "/r" to check if you have completed the Computer registration

> If you haven't registered, the bot will send you a registration link, you can complete the Mixin Computer registration by paying 0.001 XIN, wait a few minutes and then enter "/r" to check if you have completed the registration (note: do not repeat the payment link, repeated payment will not be refunded)

<Callout type='info'>
You can also register Mixin Computer at [mvm.dev](https://mvm.dev/demo), login your Mixin Messenger account to check if you have completed the registration.
</Callout>

Fluxor's first MVP version is very simple, you can watch the [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pk4algZiEww&t=1145s) to understand how to complete all the lending operations through Mixin Messager

## Steps to complete the lending operation

After entering the robot homepage, you can see the existing lending pools, each lending pool supports 存入/借款/提现/还款 operations:

![hero](https://docs.image.fluxor.cc/Fluxor%20Use%20Case/hero.png)


Click the top left corner to connect your wallet to start the lending operation:

After entering the lending pool, you can view the information of the specific lending pool, for example, the lending pool of XIN is as follows:


### Deposit

> The first time you deposit, you need to pay an additional 0.04 XIN to complete the registration of the lending account, it is recommended to deposit a small amount of XIN the first time.

> Each time you operate through Computer, you need to pay 0.001 XIN fee

Open XIN Bank:

![XIN Bank](https://docs.image.fluxor.cc/Fluxor%20Use%20Case/xin-bank-info.png)

Suppose you want to deposit 0.1 XIN:

![lend xin 0.1 01](https://docs.image.fluxor.cc/Fluxor%20Use%20Case/lend-xin-0.1-01.png)

Click Deposit:
![lend xin 0.1 02](https://docs.image.fluxor.cc/Fluxor%20Use%20Case/lend-xin-0.1-02.png)

> For the first time you deposit an asset into the lending pool, you may need to pay an additional 0.003 XIN,后续就不会了,例如:

![lend xin 0.1 03](https://docs.image.fluxor.cc/Fluxor%20Use%20Case/lend-xin-0.1-03.png)

After the operation is completed, wait a few minutes, the robot will send a notification:

![lend xin 0.1 04](https://docs.image.fluxor.cc/Fluxor%20Use%20Case/lend-xin-0.1-04.png)

When the confirmation is completed, the page will prompt that it has been completed:

![lend xin 0.1 05](https://docs.image.fluxor.cc/Fluxor%20Use%20Case/lend-xin-0.1-05.png)

At the same time, your lending portfolio interface will be updated:

![lend xin 0.1 06](https://docs.image.fluxor.cc/Fluxor%20Use%20Case/lend-xin-0.1-06.png)

The robot will also send a prompt:

![lend xin 0.1 07](https://docs.image.fluxor.cc/Fluxor%20Use%20Case/lend-xin-0.1-07.png)

> If you deposit again after the first time, you may need to pay an additional 0.04 XIN, this is probably because the account initialized for the first operation has not been loaded out, you can refresh the interface to avoid initializing the second lending account.
